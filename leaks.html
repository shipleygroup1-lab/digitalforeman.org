The 5 places contractors lose jobs

I own a screen porch company in Myrtle Beach. I tracked every Facebook lead we got, then checked what happened to each one. These are the 5 spots where jobs slipped away between the first call and the signed contract, and the fix for each.

LEAK 1

Missed calls with no text back

You're on a ladder, the phone rings, and by the time you call back they've called someone else.

Fix: an automatic text the minute you miss a call: "Sorry I missed you, this is Tom. What can I help you with?"

LEAK 2

A slow first reply

A new lead sits for hours (or until tomorrow) before anyone answers.

Fix: every new lead gets an automatic text and email right away, so they hear from you in minutes, even when you're busy.

LEAK 3

One call, then giving up

You call once, get voicemail, and the lead goes cold.

Fix: put follow-up on a schedule: call the same day, call again the next day, then text. Don't stop after one try.

LEAK 4

An estimate sent, then silence

You send the proposal and wait for them to call. Most never do.

Fix: automatic follow-ups after every estimate: short, friendly texts and emails checking in over the next couple of weeks.

LEAK 5

No review or referral ask after the job

The customer is happy, you pack up, and you never ask.

Fix: the day the job is done, send a thank-you text with your review link and ask who else they know who needs work.
