Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Digital Foreman ("we," "us") builds and manages websites and back-office systems for contractors and local service businesses. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Information you give us on our website, on a Facebook or Instagram lead form, by phone, by text or by email. This can include your name, business name, phone number, email address, trade and details about your business.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and the device or browser used. This comes from our website host and from advertising tools such as the Meta Pixel.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the service you asked about, schedule a consultation and send you a quote.
  • To provide and support the services you buy from us.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and our website.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as Meta (Facebook and Instagram), our form and email providers, our CRM, and tools such as Zapier and Google, and only to provide our services to you. We may also share it if the law requires us to.

Texts and calls

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we have about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@digitalforeman.org.
