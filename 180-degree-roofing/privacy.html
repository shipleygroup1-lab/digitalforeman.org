Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

180 Degree Roofing ("we," "us") is a locally owned roofing company serving the Concord, North Carolina area. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us, request a quote, or fill out one of our forms (including forms on Facebook and Instagram), and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address, and the street address of your property.
  • Project details: what you need (for example a roof replacement, repair, or storm damage), the age of your roof, photos you send us, and any notes you share.
  • Messages and calls: texts, emails, and calls between you and our business.
  • Website and ad data: basic information our website and advertising tools collect automatically, such as pages visited and whether you clicked one of our ads.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and prepare your roof quote, including an aerial roof measurement report.
  • To schedule estimates and roofing work, and to send reminders and updates about your project.
  • To send proposals, invoices, and warranty information.
  • To follow up about a quote you requested.
  • To improve our website and advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and agree to be contacted, we may text you about your quote, appointments, and project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. You can also call us at 800-262-0298.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only so they can do that work for us. These include our customer management and scheduling software, our business phone and texting provider, our email provider, our website host, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) when you submit one of our forms there. We may also share information when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide your quote and service, honor your warranty, and meet our business and legal record-keeping obligations.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to update or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, at any time.
  • You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and out of emails by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.

Children

Our services are for homeowners and property owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
